# Settings and data

Every setting, backups, restoring, and games that can't be read.

[Open Settings](#/settings)

## Dice

Show dice as icons or as text (d8). Rolling picks whether the app rolls for you or you roll your own dice and enter the numbers.

## Alerts

Choose what to be told about (your turn, or something that needs your answer, such as the GM answering a request or a new request for the GM) and how: a sound, a vibration, or a notification when the app is in the background.

## Display

Text size makes everything bigger. Motion follows your system's setting, or always reduces or allows animation.

## New games

Defaults for games you start from now on: the scene tracker, whether new opposition starts hidden, and how much of a villain players see.

## Backing up

Back up everything downloads one file with your whole library, your games, the games you've joined and your settings. Keep it somewhere safe; it's the only copy outside this browser.

## Restoring

Restore from a backup reads a backup file. Add to what's here keeps what you have and adds what's new. Replace everything deletes what's on this device first, and offers to back that up before it does.

## Importing

Import a hero, villain or game that someone exported or sent you as a file. If you already have it, you're asked whether to keep both or replace yours.

## Games this version can't read

After an update, an old game may no longer open. It's listed in Settings › Data with Delete, so it doesn't get in the way.
