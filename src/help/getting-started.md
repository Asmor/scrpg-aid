# Getting started

What SCRPG Aid is for, where to begin, and where your things are kept.

## Two ways to start

If you're a player, build a hero first. Once you have one, a GM can give you a game code and you join their game with that hero.

If you're the GM, open Games and make a new game. You can make villains, minions, challenges and environments ahead of time in your library, then bring them onto the table when the scene needs them.

## The library

The library is the home screen. It holds everything you make: heroes, villains, and the GM's templates for minions, lieutenants, challenges and environments. Tag things however suits you (by game, by group, by scene) and filter by those tags to find them. A search you use often can be saved.

## Games

The Games page lists the games you run on this device and the games you've joined as a player. A game holds one table: its scenes, the turn order, and copies of the heroes and villains in it. Changing a hero in your library doesn't change the copy in a game until you choose to update it.

## What's saved where

Everything is saved in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you host or join a game, or send items to someone. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it all, so back up now and then from Settings › Data. The app reminds you when it's been a while.

[Open Settings](#/settings)

## Working offline

Once the app has loaded, it keeps working without a connection: building heroes, running a game on one device, and reading games you joined earlier. Hosting and joining need a connection.
