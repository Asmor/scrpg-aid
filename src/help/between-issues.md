# Between issues

What happens when the GM finishes an issue: back issues, collections, hero points and advancement.

## Back issues

When the GM finishes an issue, each player is asked to add it to their hero's back issues. Add it and the issue goes on the hero in your library. Later closes the prompt, and the issue waits on the hero's Advancement page under Not recorded yet.

## Collections

A hero's back issues can be gathered into a collection. When the prompt or the Advancement page offers it, give the collection a name and record it.

## Hero points

Hero points belong to the table, not to one hero. Between issues, spend them on bonuses for your hero for the next issue; the GM's table shows what's been bought, and anything you change your mind about can be returned before the next issue starts.

## Advancement

A hero's Advancement page holds its back issues and collections, the swaps and the major rewrite they unlock, and a history of every change. Open it from the hero's page.

## The next issue

Finishing an issue clears every hero's bonuses and penalties. The GM starts the next issue when everyone's ready, choosing what resets, and play resumes.
