# Glossary

The words the app uses, and what each one means here.

## Back issue

An issue a hero took part in, recorded on the hero. Back issues are what advancement is built on.

## Collection

A named set of a hero's back issues, recorded together.

## Game

One table: its scenes and issues, the turn order, and copies of the heroes, villains and NPCs in it. It's saved on the GM's device.

## Hidden

On the table but not shown to players. Hidden things don't count for anything until they're revealed.

## Issue

One story within a game, made of scenes. Finishing an issue pauses play until the next one starts.

## Library

Everything you've made, on this device: heroes, villains and templates.

## Revealed

On the table and shown to players. Opposition that started hidden is revealed when the GM chooses.

## Scene

One part of an issue, with its own tracker and its own opposition on the table.

## Set aside

Opposition moved off the table at the end of a scene, kept with the game for later.

## Side

Who a character acts for on the table: the heroes, the GM, or the environment.

## Solo game

A hero's or villain's own play state on its Play page, used without a GM or a hosted game.

## Template

A minion, lieutenant, challenge or environment in your library, copied onto the table each time it's brought in.
