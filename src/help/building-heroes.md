# Building heroes

The hero wizard, drafts, and changing a hero once it's finished.

## The wizard

New › Hero opens the wizard. It goes through the steps of making a hero in order, one page each, and checks the rules as you go. When a choice breaks a rule, the step shows a warning saying what's wrong; it doesn't stop you, so you can sort it out with your table.

Each step has a summary on the side, and the sheet preview shows the hero as it stands.

## Rolling or choosing

Most steps let you roll on the step's table or pick from it yourself. Rolls use the app's dice, or your own dice if Settings › Dice › Rolling is set to physical dice.

## Drafts

A hero is a draft until you finish the wizard. Drafts are saved as you go and show in the library with a Draft tag. Opening a draft continues where you left off.

## Special archetypes

A few archetypes have more to them than the rest, such as a hero with two forms or one built from interchangeable parts. Their step is split into parts, and the wizard walks you through each part before moving on.

## Editing a finished hero

Edit, on a finished hero's page, opens a summary of every step; open a step to change it.

## Advancement

Changes a hero earns through play (back issues, collections, swaps and the major rewrite) are on the hero's Advancement page, not in the editor. See Between issues.

## Sheets and exports

The hero's page shows the sheet. Export it as a PDF to print, as Markdown, or as a file you can import on another device or share.
