# Villains and NPCs

Making the GM's side: villains, and templates for minions, lieutenants, challenges and environments.

## The villain wizard

New › Villain opens the villain wizard. It goes step by step: the villain's approach, its archetype, upgrades, mastery and Health. As with heroes, it warns about rule problems without stopping you.

## Suggest 3

On the wizard's main choices (approach, archetype, upgrades, mastery), Suggest 3 shows three random options instead of the whole list, for inspiration. It isn't an official way to make a villain. Re-roll for three more, or Show all to go back to the full list; nothing you've chosen changes either way.

## Templates

Minions, lieutenants, challenges and environments are templates. You make one once in the library, then bring it onto the table as often as you like; each time, the game gets its own copy.

## Minions and lieutenants

A minion or lieutenant template is one kind of NPC. On the table they come in groups: bring in three thugs and the group card tracks each of them, rolls for them together, and handles their saves when they're hit.

## Challenges

A challenge is a set of steps the heroes work through. On the table, players roll Overcome against it and the GM marks steps done. A challenge belongs to the GM or to the environment.

## Environments

An environment template describes where a scene takes place, and can bring its own minions, lieutenants and challenges with it. A game has at most one environment at a time, and it takes its own turn.
