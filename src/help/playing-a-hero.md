# Playing a hero

Using your hero at the table, in someone's game or on your own.

## Joining a game

Play together › Join as a player, then enter the code your GM gave you and pick a hero. The game gets a copy of your hero's sheet. If you change the hero in your library later, the game offers to send the GM the update.

## Playing solo

A hero's Play page works without a GM, for trying things out or for a game where everyone shares one device. It keeps its own play state, separate from any game.

## Rolling

Pick an action and the dice to use, then roll. The app adds up the result, applies your bonuses and penalties, and records the roll. A toast offers Undo for a few seconds afterwards.

## Physical dice

If you'd rather roll real dice, set Settings › Dice › Rolling to physical dice. The app then asks for each die's number instead of rolling.

## Dice pad

With physical dice, the dice pad is where you enter your roll: tap the numbers you rolled, in any order, then Accept. Roll for me hands the roll back to the app.

## Bonuses and penalties

Bonuses and penalties are listed on your hero and offered when you roll. A persistent one stays after you use it; any other is used up by the roll that uses it. An exclusive one can't be stacked with another exclusive one of the same kind, so the roll lets you check only one of each.

## Reactions

A reaction can be used outside your turn. Once used, it's marked until it's ready again, and the table tells you when it is.

## Forms and modes

If your hero has forms or modes, switch between them on the Play page. The sheet shows what the current one gives you.

## Overcome against a challenge

When a challenge is on the table, roll Overcome on it from your table. The result goes to the GM, who marks the step done.

## Hero points

Hero points belong to the whole table. They're shown on the table during play and can be spent between issues. See Between issues.
